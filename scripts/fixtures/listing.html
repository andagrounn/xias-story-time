Age 4-6

Read the best free bedtime stories, fairy tales, and books for kindergarten and school aged kids online!

The Window Seat

Two sisters compete for a train’s window seat, but a thoughtful act brings them a view to share in this gentle story about kindness and family.

Long-Legged Bob

A tall boy discovers confidence, friendship and adventure when rock climbing leads him into a magical world where his long legs become a strength.

The Circus School

Four friends join a travelling circus school, discover unexpected talents, and learn how to practice.

The OTHER Superheroes

A funny story about some messy superheroes… and the REAL superheroes who clean up behind them!

Ruby

Brie adopts Ruby, an imperfect puppy, while her friend Nate learns what it’s like to feel different.

My Own Rainforest

A lyrical rainforest meditation for kids about exploring a secret mountain forest and swimming in a sparkling waterfall.

Pobblebonk!

Pobblebonks, puggles and wobbegongs live in Australia! How can one community help wildlife thrive?

Dawn Elizabeth Daly’s Pet

A little girl discovers that love can grow in unexpected ways after finding – and losing – her unusual pet tortoise.

Mom’s Hands

A gentle bedtime story introducing sign language and love through everyday moments between a mother and child.
